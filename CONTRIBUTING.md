# Contributing

Thank you for your interest. This is an individually maintained project.

- **Welcome:** questions, ideas and corrections through GitHub Issues and Discussions.
- **By invitation:** pull requests are accepted only after the maintainer has agreed to them in an issue first.
- **Rights:** by submitting a contribution you agree that it may be used, modified and distributed by the maintainer as part of this project, under the project license, and that you have the right to submit it.
- **Credit:** accepted contributions are credited in `CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Scope:** keep examples illustrative. Please do not submit confidential, employer or customer data.
